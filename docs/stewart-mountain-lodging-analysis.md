## The business at a glance

- **Who**: Stewart Mountain Lodging, run by Brad and Vicki, full-time
  residents of the canyon. The Stewart family homesteaded the canyon in the
  1890s and descendants opened the original Timp Haven ski resort (which
  became Sundance).
- **Where**: Sundance, Utah (Provo Canyon area). Phone: 801-229-1111.
- **What**: 40+ hand-curated luxury cabin/cottage vacation rentals, plus an
  event venue ("The Hollow" — private guest-only mountain park with
  streamside firepit and zip line), concierge services, real estate
  listings, and property management for cabin owners.
- **Reputation**: 4.9 stars across 300+ verified stays; reviews repeatedly
  praise cleanliness and responsiveness. Cleanliness is already a core part
  of their brand promise.

## Operational facts relevant to Liz's cleaning services

- **Turnover window is tight**: check-out 11:00 AM, check-in 4:00 PM — a
  5-hour window. FAQ explicitly says "Cleaners arrive promptly" at 11.
- **They clean in-house today**: professional cleaning after every
  departure, with "nearly 20 years" of developed cleaning systems, plus an
  in-house maintenance department and their own snow-removal equipment.
  Any partnership is likely augmentation/overflow/specialty, not takeover.
- **Daily housekeeping is an upsell**: FAQ offers daily linen service for
  bedding and towels for guests who want it — an existing paid-service
  hook Liz could staff or expand.
- **High-touch surfaces everywhere**: hot tubs at most properties, saunas,
  multiple wood-burning fireplaces (ash removal), gourmet kitchens (Wolf
  ranges, Sub-Zero fridges), luxury linens, delicate antiques and custom
  furnishings that need trained handling.
- **Deep-clean revenue exists**: $1,000 smoking violation triggers deep
  cleaning; $49/reservation damage waiver covers up to $1,500; pet-friendly
  units ($35/night/dog) mean fur/dander protocols.
- **Seasonal complications**: 4WD/chains required Nov 1–Mar 31 — cleaners
  need capable vehicles in winter; some units have no on-site laundry, so
  linens are hauled to a central laundry.
- **Events**: The Hollow venue and approved gatherings ($1,000+/day) imply
  event-scale post-party cleans.

### Cleaning-service opportunity ideas

## Website technical notes (improvement pitch)

- **Stack**: WordPress with "Bizcor Luxury" theme, integrated with
  **Streamline VRS** booking software (search widget, booking calendar,
  owner login). Property pages at `/lodging/<slug>/`.
- **What works**: strong photography (39–50 photos per listing), booking
  calendar with live pricing, good filter set (hot tub, sauna, EV charger,
  pet-friendly, Starlink, game rooms, map search).
- **Weak spots observed**:
  - The linked search-results URL (`/search-results/?sort_by=random&plus_oc=1`)
    renders "no inventory available" — a dead-feeling landing state for a
    URL that's apparently being shared around.
  - Pricing and cleaning fees are hidden until dates are entered — friction
    and a possible trust issue for comparison shoppers.
  - Several listings have raw numeric slugs (`/lodging/941224/`) — bad for
    SEO and sharing.
  - Some inconsistent URL casing/naming (`Mandan-37-Upper`,
    `river_song_cottage`) suggests hand-entered content drift.
  - No dedicated cleanliness/housekeeping page even though reviews and
    their own pitch center on it — a "Our 20-Year Cleaning Standard" page
    would convert well and could feature a partner like Liz.
- **Content**: active blog (Sundance Festival lodging tips, etc.),
  Facebook/Instagram links, gift certificates, pet-friendly landing page,
  real estate section.
